Yesterday I attended a great **Tech & Meet** session featuring AI researcher and engineer [Niels Rogge](https://www.linkedin.com/in/niels-rogge-a3b7a3127/).

It was super interesting to see how AI is being leveraged in the cybersecurity space, and honestly eye-opening to realise just how powerful these models are becoming when it comes to identifying software vulnerabilities.

## What we covered

Using the Mythos case as a starting point, Niels walked us through:

- What open-source AI models are capable of today in vulnerability detection.
- The reality of AI security risks versus the hype in current headlines.
- How discussions around AI safety, regulation and open source are often as much about business strategy as they are about technology.

## Takeaway

It was a fantastic, practical and evidence-based look at where modern AI is actually headed. Big thanks to Niels and the organisers for a really insightful event!

## Exploring Internship Opportunities

Today, I attended the **Howest Internship Fair (Stage Markt)** to meet companies and learn more about their internship opportunities for the upcoming semester.

As a third-year Applied Computer Science student specialising in Software Engineering at Howest, this was a great opportunity to connect with companies, discover what they are working on, and explore potential directions for my final internship.

![Howest Internship Fair]({{ '/assets/images/howest-internship-fair.jpg' | relative_url }})

*An impression of the Howest Internship Fair.*

## Meeting companies and discovering opportunities

During the event, I had interesting conversations with several companies:

- [**Volvo Group**](https://www.linkedin.com/company/volvo-group/) — opportunities involving full-stack development, .NET, cloud technologies and logistics systems.
- [**Citymesh**](https://www.linkedin.com/company/citymesh/) — an opportunity to learn more about their projects and the technologies they use.
- **Vanden Broele** — a chance to discover their activities and potential software development opportunities.
- [**C&W Logistics**](https://www.linkedin.com/company/c-w-logistics/) — insights into logistics software and the role technology plays in business operations.

It was interesting to hear about the different projects, technologies and challenges these companies work with. Each conversation provided a different perspective on how software engineering is applied in a professional environment.

## Technologies that caught my attention

Several topics particularly interested me, including **.NET, cloud technologies, full-stack development and logistics software**.

These areas offer opportunities to apply the knowledge I have gained throughout my studies while continuing to develop my technical skills. I am especially interested in finding an internship where I can contribute to real projects, learn from experienced developers and gain practical experience in a professional development team.

## Looking ahead

The internship fair was a valuable opportunity to explore the possibilities available for my final internship next semester. Speaking directly with companies helped me better understand the kinds of projects and working environments I could see myself joining.

My next step is to evaluate the opportunities and determine which internship best matches my interests, skills and ambitions as a future software engineer.

Thank you to all the companies for the interesting conversations and for taking the time to explain their work and internship opportunities!
